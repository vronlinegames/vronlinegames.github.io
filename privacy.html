Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VR Oniline Games ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our VR games on the Meta Horizon Store, including Pop Together (the "Game"). The Game is a single-player game.

1. Who We Are

VR Oniline Games is an independent game developer. This policy is owned and maintained by VR Oniline Games. You can contact us at lenovostudio289@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or receive any personal information from players. The Game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, voice, or camera data.

The Game does not include our own accounts, analytics, tracking, or advertising, and it does not send any data to servers operated by us.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The Game may save game progress and settings locally on your headset so you can continue playing. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us.

4. Meta Horizon Platform

The Game runs on the Meta Horizon platform. Meta may process information such as your Meta account ID to confirm that you own the Game (entitlement check) and to provide platform features. This processing is done by Meta under the Meta Privacy Policy. Meta may also provide us with aggregated, non-identifying reports such as install counts and crash statistics.

5. Payments

If you buy the Game or any content in it, payment is handled entirely by Meta. We never receive or store your payment details.

6. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not use it for any purpose. Aggregated reports from Meta are used only to fix bugs and improve the Game.

7. Sharing and Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties.

8. Data Retention

We do not keep any personal information. Data saved on your device remains there until you delete it or uninstall the Game.

9. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You can delete all Game data stored on your device at any time by uninstalling the Game. If you have any question about your data, or want to make a deletion or access request, email us at lenovostudio289@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Age requirements for the Game follow the Meta Horizon Store's terms.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the Game changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
